Severity distribution i Each observation is classified by its Congestion Level: Critical (above 90), High (75-90), Moderate (50-75), or Low (below 50). This shows how often Bangalore roads fall into each band.
The core finding
52.3% of all observations fall in the Critical band (congestion above 90). The top 5 roads are at full road capacity 89.8% of the time. This is not peak-hour overload -- it is structural saturation that persists across all days and most weather conditions. Five roads (Sony World Junction, Sarjapur Road, Anil Kumble Circle, Trinity Circle, CMH Road) account for 52.3% of total measured traffic volume while averaging a congestion score of 91.5, which is 13.2% above the city mean.
