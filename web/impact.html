Economic Impact i These numbers estimate the daily cost of congestion delays at Bangalore's top 5 chokepoints. Two costs are counted: time lost by commuters (productivity) and extra fuel burned while idling. The estimates use average daily traffic volumes from the dataset combined with standard wage and fuel assumptions.

What congestion at the top 5 roads costs the city every single day.

estimated daily economic loss -- top 5 chokepoints
Every commuter stuck in traffic for 15 minutes loses Rs 37.50 in productive time and wastes Rs 20.60 in fuel. Multiplied across ~1.85 lakh vehicles per day at these five roads, the total reaches over a crore of rupees -- every day.
