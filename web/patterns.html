The pattern finding
Congestion in Bangalore does not follow a typical commuter curve. Weekday and weekend congestion levels track within 2-3 points of each other across all months. Rain and Overcast conditions record congestion below the Clear-weather baseline of 80.72, while Windy weather produces the highest reading (82.37 -- only 1.65 points above Clear). The data points to one conclusion: the overload is structural and road-capacity-driven, not time-sensitive or weather-sensitive.
